A little help

Good to know.

Can I buy these products?

This is a demo storefront. The products, prices, and stock levels are sample data. Checkout demonstrates the flow without creating a real order or taking payment.

How does the bag work?

Choose a product, select a size, and add it to your bag. You can update quantities or remove items. Your bag is saved in this browser when local storage is available; another device or browser has its own bag.

How much is delivery?

For the demo, delivery is R80.00 and becomes free when the subtotal reaches R1,500.00. Actual delivery areas, fees, and timeframes must be confirmed before the store goes live.

How do I choose a size?

The displayed sizes are samples. Product pages describe the intended fit, but there is no verified measurement chart yet. A real size guide should be added with the final catalogue.

Where is the boutique?

The website is inspired by Cape Town, South Africa. The map shows Cape Town generally. A physical store address and trading hours have not yet been supplied.

What is the returns policy?

This demonstration does not accept real orders. A confirmed returns and refund policy must be published before live sales begin.

Can I reduce the motion?

Yes. Use Pause motion on the home page, or enable reduced motion in your device settings. Navigation and shopping remain usable with motion disabled.

Does the contact form send my message?

No. It downloads an enquiry text file to your device. An email address or working form service must be configured before enquiries can be sent.
