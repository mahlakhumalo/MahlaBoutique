The practical details

Clear by design.

About this demonstration

MAHLA Boutique is a concept storefront. Products, prices, stock, and delivery fees are illustrative. No purchases, payments, reservations, or binding orders are processed.

Browser storage

The bag and motion preference are stored in local storage on this device. A demo order reference and product totals may be stored in session storage, which lasts for the browser session. Checkout names, addresses, and email addresses are not saved or sent.

Enquiry downloads

The enquiry form creates a text file on your device. Its contents are not sent to the store. Delete the downloaded file if you no longer need it.

Google Maps

The embedded map loads only after you choose “Load interactive map”. Google then receives the connection information needed to serve the map. You can instead open the external Google Maps link. See Google’s privacy policy for details.

Images and catalogue

Campaign and product images are illustrative generated visuals, not photographs of verified stock. Product materials, measurements, and care requirements have not been confirmed.

Before live sales

Business identity and contact details, genuine stock, verified prices and tax treatment, delivery terms, a returns policy, a privacy notice appropriate to the business, and a secure payment service must be configured before taking orders.

Your controls

Remove products on the bag page to clear your cart. Clear this site’s browser storage to reset all saved preferences. No analytics or marketing cookies are added by this website’s code.
