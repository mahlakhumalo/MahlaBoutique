# Image notes

All included images are illustrative generated assets, not verified inventory or photographs of actual customers. No old Costal Threads logo or unrelated catalogue pictures are included. All image assets are local, optimised WebP files.

- campaign.webp: generated landscape fashion editorial with two adult Black South African models wearing a flowing taupe dress and charcoal suit on a rocky Cape-inspired coast, overcast sky and subdued natural tones, no text or logo.
- campaign-foreground.webp: transparent edit of the campaign image isolating the models for layered homepage lettering.
- dress.webp: generated full-length portrait of an adult woman wearing a flowing stone halter maxi dress against a dark textured wall.
- suit.webp: generated full-length portrait of an adult man in an oversized charcoal two-piece suit against a pale textured wall.
- tote.webp: generated black shoulder tote on a travertine plinth with warm-grey background.
- linen.webp: existing generated coastal editorial reused from the user-selected Costal Threads reference; woman in ivory shirt and taupe trousers by sunlit coastal architecture.

The built-in image generator was used for the new campaign, catalogue portraits, tote, and foreground edit. No external image model CLI was used. The brief consistently requested understated fashion photography, realistic fabric, muted charcoal/stone/ivory tones, no text, no logos, and no watermarks.

Local font: Inter, carried with its licence from the reference website. Serif accents use system Georgia.
